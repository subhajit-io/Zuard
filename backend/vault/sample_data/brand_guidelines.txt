ZUARD OFFICIAL BRAND BOOK v2
Pre-Publication Brand Governance & Editorial Standards
Published: January 2026

Section 1.0: Brand Mission & Persona
Zuard exists to empower scaling enterprises and digital creators to publish content with 100% confidence, velocity, and regulatory compliance. Our brand personality is authoritative, innovative, supportive, and precise. We speak with quiet confidence, eschewing sensationalism, hype, or baseless boasting.

=== PAGE 2 ===
Section 1.1: Core Tone Principles
1. Authoritative Clarity: Every statement should be substantiated, concise, and direct. Avoid passive fluff.
2. Constructive Empathy: When addressing users or communicating features, focus on unlocking creator productivity, not policing or penalizing.
3. Technical Precision: Use accurate technical terminology. Refer to algorithmic decisions with transparency.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Section 1.2: Reading Level & Sentence Complexity
All external marketing copy, documentation, and blog posts must target a reading grade level between Grade 8 and Grade 11 (Flesch Reading Ease score between 55.0 and 75.0). Paragraphs should rarely exceed four sentences. Dense academic jargon that obscures practical value is prohibited.

=== PAGE 4 ===
Section 1.3: Pricing Persona & Vocabulary
When discussing our commercial tiers, use value-focused, professional terms such as "cost-effective", "budget-friendly", "streamlined investment", or "high-ROI deployment". Terms like "super cheap", "dirt cheap", or "cut-rate" degrade enterprise brand equity and are strictly forbidden.

=== PAGE 5 ===
Section 1.4: Modern Technical Tone
We reject sensationalist buzzwords. Never describe software features as "miracle solutions", "insane hacks", or "revolutionary game-changers". Instead, characterize capabilities as "innovative", "high-velocity", "deeply engineered", or "state-of-the-art".

=== PAGE 6 ===
Section 1.5: Professional Editorial Standards
Marketing and sales enablement documents must avoid colloquial internet slang (e.g., "life hack", "killer feature", "crushing it"). All claims must sound credible in an enterprise boardroom or legal audit.

=== PAGE 7 ===
Section 2.1: Legal & Regulatory Disclaimers
Under pre-publication advertising governance, the word "guaranteed" must never be used in isolation for marketing copy without citing our formal Service Level Agreement (SLA). Use "designed to achieve" or "backed by our 99.95% SLA commitment" instead.

=== PAGE 8 ===
Section 2.2: Verifiable Market Claims
Superlative market rankings such as "#1", "number one", "world's best", or "fastest platform" require formal third-party benchmark certification from recognized analysts (e.g., Gartner, IDC, Forrester). Unverified superlative claims generate immediate compliance violations.

=== PAGE 9 ===
Section 2.3: Factual Consistency Policy
All published numerical metrics, customer counts, and cost-reduction percentages must directly correlate with entries in the Approved Facts Database. Authors may not fabricate or extrapolate statistics for rhetorical effect.

=== PAGE 10 ===
Section 3.4: Nomenclature & Product Lifecycles
Refer to products solely by their official current release titles:
- The governance engine is "Zuard Enterprise Platform" (the legacy name "BrandX Cloud v1" is permanently retired).
- The hybrid search pipeline is "Branched RAG Gateway" (the older designation "legacy sync" is deprecated).

=== PAGE 11 ===
Section 4.1: Official Benchmarks & Performance Claims
Official benchmark verified by internal QA:
- Automated pre-screening turnaround time: 2.5 seconds end-to-end.
- Compute reduction over full-document LLM queries: 85% reduction via Delta-Only architecture.
- Manual manager fact-checking reduction: 70% faster review cycles.

=== PAGE 12 ===
Section 4.2: Financial Impact Metrics
The only verified, auditable cost-reduction metric approved for public distribution is: "Demonstrated 42% average operational cost reduction across enterprise deployments (Study ID #F-104)". Any claim asserting 90%, 99%, or 99.9% cost reductions is factually contradictory and prohibited.

=== PAGE 13 ===
Section 5.0: Visual Identity & Color Harmony
Zuard's visual identity relies on high-contrast, modernist digital aesthetics. Our official primary color is Brand Navy (#0F172A), complemented by Electric Blue (#2563EB) and Cyan Accent (#06B6D4). Unauthorized vibrant hues such as Hot Magenta (#FF0055) conflict with brand identity and are banned from promotional banners.

=== PAGE 14 ===
Section 5.1: WCAG Accessibility Standards
All digital creative collateral, banners, infographics, and PDF documents must conform to WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standards:
- Normal body copy against background: Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
- Large headers (18pt+ or 14pt+ bold): Minimum 3.0:1 contrast ratio.
- High-priority regulatory notices must target AAA standard (7.0:1).
All uploaded promotional graphics must include comprehensive, descriptive alt-text.

=== PAGE 15 ===
Section 5.2: Visual Asset Protection & Geometry
The official Zuard horizontal logo possesses a fixed 3.5:1 aspect ratio with 16px minimum clear space on all sides. Stretching, squashing, or rotating the logo mark beyond 5% distortion is an explicit violation.
