ZUARD CONSUMER & MOBILE BRAND BOOK 2026
Creative Governance for Mobile App & Social Creators

Section 1.0: Consumer Persona
Zuard Consumer is lively, inspiring, fast, and empowering. We design for digital creators, streamers, and mobile video editors who value agility, expression, and joy.

=== PAGE 2 ===
Section 1.1: Engaging Creator Voice
Keep sentence structures snappy and energetic. Reading grade level should be between Grade 6 and Grade 9. Avoid dry corporate jargon like 'enterprise operational overhead'. Terms like 'super cheap' degrade our design reputation; use 'accessible' or 'budget-friendly'.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Section 1.2: Creator Community Scale
Our verified benchmark is: 'Over 5 million active mobile creators using the Zuard Mobile suite (Fact #FC-201)'. Do not claim 10M or 20M without updated product analytics sign-off.

=== PAGE 4 ===
Section 2.1: Mobile Engine Benchmarks
Mobile audio/video sync achieves a certified 50ms latency across 5G networks (Fact #FC-202).

=== PAGE 5 ===
Section 3.0: Consumer Tier Specifications
The Free starter plan provides 10 full-resolution exports per month. Do not market the app as 'unlimited free forever' as that misleads users regarding cloud processing limits.

=== PAGE 6 ===
Section 4.0: Visual Identity & Color Energy
Zuard Consumer uses an energetic color palette: Electric Violet (#8B5CF6) and Sunset Amber (#F59E0B) complemented by Neon Teal (#10B981). Muddy earth tones (#556B2F) are strictly prohibited.
