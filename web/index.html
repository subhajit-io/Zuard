Zuard Architecture & Beginner's Guide

1

Workspace Isolation & Guidelines Ingestion

Each workspace has its own brand persona, color rules, and verified facts. You can drop a brand guidelines PDF into the Guidelines & Rules tab at any time to dynamically re-instruct workers.

2

4 Concurrent Static Workers (~2–4ms, $0 LLM Cost)

Runs deterministic static checks in parallel before invoking any generative model:

  • Worker #1 (Lexical & Tone): Fast terminology scanning + readability analysis.
  • Worker #2 (Facts & Claims): SQLite index lookup verifying numbers, stats, and market metrics.
  • Worker #3 (Visual & Palette): CIELAB ΔE perceptual color distance verification.
  • Worker #4 (WCAG 2.1 Math): Mathematical relative luminance contrast ratio verification.
3

Delta-Only Synthesizer with Branched RAG Citations

If zero violations occur, 0 tokens are spent. If violations are detected, only the flagged snippets are processed with Branched RAG, citing exact page numbers, section headers, and study IDs with zero hallucinations.

4

Human Review & Official Submission Clearance

Reviewers can accept intent-preserving rewrites with 1 click or override with justified rationale. Once approved, an auditable clearance certificate with SHA256 hash is generated.
