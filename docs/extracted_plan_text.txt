1. PROBLEM DEFINITION & EVIDENCE THE PROBLEM IS REAL
1.1 The Core Problem
Growing organisations publish content at high velocity through internal employees and external marketing
agencies. Because brand rules live in static, disconnected PDF files and spreadsheets, content frequently goes live
without unified verification. This creates brand inconsistency and credibility damage across six critical
dimensions:
#
Dimension
What goes wrong
1
Inconsistent Tone & Brand Voice
Copy written by different employees or agencies drifts away from the organisation's
approved persona.
2
Banned & Inconsistent Terminology
Use of deprecated product names, unapproved industry jargon, or restricted
promotional buzzwords (e.g., "cheap", "guaranteed").
3
Outdated Factual Information
Publishing legacy pricing tiers, retired feature specifications, or outdated company
metrics.
4
Unsupported Promotional Claims
Making unverifiable superlative claims (e.g., "#1 fastest platform", "99.9% cost
reduction") without approved source backing.
5
Off-Brand Visual Consistency
Marketing banners and promotional graphics using unapproved Hex color codes or
stretched logos.
6
Accessibility Non-Compliance
Publishing visuals and documents that fail WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA color-contrast
standards (minimum 4.5:1) or omit alt-text.

3. PUBLIC SOCIAL MEDIA DISCUSSIONS & COMMUNITY
EVIDENCE
 An analysis of public social media posts and community discussions across Reddit (r/marketing, r/copywriting,
r/graphic_design), LinkedIn marketing forums, and X/Twitter threads reveals widespread, real-world operational
friction caused by static brand guidelines and manual approval bottlenecks:
1. Frustration with Static PDFs & Disrupted Creative Flow (Reddit & X/Twitter Discussions)

Creators frequently post about ignoring 50+ page PDF style guides because searching through complex PDF
documents while drafting content severely interrupts writing momentum.

Marketing professionals on LinkedIn regularly advocate for centralized automated tools that pre-screen drafts
and offer instant, line-by-line compliance checks before manager submission.
2. Reviewer Fatigue & Slow Review Cycles (LinkedIn & Marketing Forum Records)

Brand managers report spending hours weekly manually flagging repetitive errors, including deprecated
terminology, unverified superlatives, off-brand color Hex codes, and low-contrast text overlays.

Discussions emphasize that outright rejections create friction; reviewers need systems that explain rule
violations and provide constructive, compliant rewrite alternatives.
3. Widespread Brand Drift in Published Digital Content (Search & Social Media Benchmarks)

Public content audits discussed across design and marketing forums show high rates of unverified promotional
claims and promotional graphics failing WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standards (4.5:1) when published without
automated verification.

5.2 The Three Named Improvements (Tracked Across Rounds 2 & 3)
To directly align with the Round 2 (15 pts) and Round 3 (20 pts) judging rubrics, Team Xnihilo defines three
high-impact improvements upfront:
1
Improvement 1 – Centralized Gateway Web Portal: a clean, lightning-fast web app where creators drop
documents/images for instant RAG audit without needing complex browser extensions.
2
Improvement 2 – Deterministic Visual & WCAG Accessibility Engine: combines OpenCV/Pillow
K-Means color clustering (ΔE color distance against approved Hex palettes), perceptual logo hashing,
EasyOCR banner text scanning, and mathematical WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA contrast verification.
3
Improvement 3 – Confidential Local-Edge Privacy Mode & Governance Ambiguity Detector: lets
organisations toggle from Cloud Gemini APIs to 100% local execution (Ollama / WebLLM) for sensitive
pre-release materials, while automatically flagging brand guidelines that trigger high creator override rates as
"Unclear Guidelines Needs Revision."

6. RESPONSIBLE-DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS: PRIVACY, SECURITY
& HUMAN OVERSIGHT
 1. Protecting Confidential Brand Materials
Pre-release product announcements and embargoed campaigns require strict data protection. First, our Delta-Only
Architecture ensures that full confidential documents do not need to be sent to external LLMs – only isolated
sentences flagged by local static workers are transmitted. Second, organisations can switch on Confidential Local
Mode (Ollama / WebLLM) with one click, keeping 100% of embeddings, static checks, and LLM synthesis inside
their own local hardware with zero external network calls.
2. Explicit Disclosure of AI & External Services (Rule 7 & Submission Integrity)

In-Product Disclosure: Every audit card clearly displays which deterministic worker flagged the issue and
which AI model (e.g., Google Gemini 2.5 Flash or Local Ollama) generated the suggested correction, along
with the confidence score and source citation.

Development-Time AI & Tool Disclosure: During the official competition build window, Team Xnihilo will
use Google Gemini models and Antigravity available models/IDE tools to accelerate code generation, UI
component scaffolding, and debugging. In strict compliance with our Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) engineering
policy, no AI-generated code is blindly committed: every API route, RAG pipeline, static algorithm, and UI
component is manually reviewed, integration-tested, and verified by the responsible team member.
3. Human Oversight & Non-Destructive Control
Zuard is designed as a collaborative co-pilot, never an automated censor. The platform never
auto-overwrites creator text or blocks publication without human review. Creators can exercise Override with
Justification when breaking a stylistic rule intentionally (e.g., a creative campaign slogan), passing their reasoning
directly to the human Brand Manager for final judgment.
4. Security, Access Control & Accessibility
Role-Based Access Control (Creator, Reviewer, Manager) and Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) isolate
organisational brand vaults and audit logs, while our own web interface adheres to WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and
keyboard navigation standards.
